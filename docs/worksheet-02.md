# Worksheet 02 — Strings and parsing

## Goals

- Convert a phrase to word frequencies using standard-library helpers.
- Detect palindromes while ignoring spaces and punctuation.
- Normalize inconsistent whitespace in human-readable data.
- Compare two texts without changing the original strings.

### Practice data

Use data/fixtures/sample-notes.txt and test with accented letters,
numbers, mixed case, repeated separators, and empty lines.

### Questions

What is the difference between lower() and casefold()? Why might
punctuation removal be inappropriate for a source-code parser?
